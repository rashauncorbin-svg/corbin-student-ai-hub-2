Reconstruction: Promise and Limits

Examine the competing visions for rebuilding the United States after the Civil War.

◷ 25–35 minutes▤ Reading + analysis◈ CLS learning sequence
IN THIS LESSON01   Set the purpose02   Build context03   Process evidence04   Defend a claim05   Reflect
01 / LEARN IT

Start with the essential question

To what extent did Reconstruction fulfill the promises of freedom and citizenship after the Civil War?

Reconstruction was the period after the Civil War when the United States confronted the challenge of rebuilding the South and defining freedom for formerly enslaved people. The period raised fundamental questions about citizenship, federal power, political participation, and the meaning of equality.

Before you move on
